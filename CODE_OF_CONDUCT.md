# Code of Conduct

Steno welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and communication styles.
- Protect privacy and use synthetic data when discussing recordings or transcripts.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disagreements without harassment or retaliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Steno project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Lead Maintainer.
If no other private channel is available, use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting form and identify the report as a conduct concern.
Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

The Lead Maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary.
If the Lead Maintainer has a conflict of interest, the repository owner will handle the report.
