# Governance

Steno uses lightweight maintainer-led governance while the project is in public beta.

## Roles

The Lead Maintainer owns day-to-day technical direction, issue triage, pull-request review, merges, releases, and security coordination.
The current maintainer list is recorded in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md).

Contributors propose changes through issues and pull requests.
A contribution does not create an ongoing review, release, security, or governance obligation.

Skrape Limited is the copyright holder named in the project license.
Changes to the project license, copyright notice, or project name require its approval in addition to the normal technical review.

## Decisions

Routine decisions are made through public issue and pull-request discussion.
The Lead Maintainer seeks consensus where practical and makes the final decision when consensus is not reached.

Changes affecting recording integrity, immutable originals, privacy boundaries, credentials, or provenance require explicit review against the architectural invariants in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Security reports are handled privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

## Becoming a maintainer

A new maintainer must demonstrate sustained, high-quality participation and sound judgment around recording safety and privacy.
The Lead Maintainer and the candidate must both explicitly accept the appointment before `MAINTAINERS.md` is changed.

Maintainer status may be removed after resignation, prolonged inactivity, or conduct that endangers the project or its users.
